Read this before you Google anything

On 1 April 2026 Ibiza scrapped its whole bus system. ALSA took over and every old line number was deleted and replaced with letters: T trunk, P beach, D Discobus, N night, AERO airport, A rural feeder, U urban. Every blog, forum and travel guide still lists the old numbers — so does the hotel's own website. Everything below is from ALSA's own 2026 timetables, valid 1 May–30 Sept.

Always tap a contactless card. Card fares are roughly 30% cheaper than cash on every line, and the multi-day tourist passes people talk about don't exist — the travel cards are residents-only.

02
Wednesday 2 September

Land, drop bags, walk to the good bar

You land at 19:25, so the sunset (20:23) is gone before you clear baggage. Don't fight it — get to the hotel, get changed, and walk six minutes to the best low-key spot in the Bay.

See this day on the map ↗
  • 19:25

    Wheels down at IBZ

    Passport, bags, out. Realistically landing-side clear by 19:50–20:10.

  • 20:30

    AERO1 to San Antonio

    Straight from the terminal. Fifty minutes, and it drops at San Antonio bus station.

  • 21:25

    The last 3.5 km

    The hotel is in Cala de Bou, not San Antonio town. The P2 bay-road bus runs hourly until 23:30; otherwise it's a short taxi. Several booking sites claim "10 minutes to the centre" — they're measuring straight across the water.

  • 22:00

    Kumharas

    Six minutes on foot. Bohemian beachside bar-restaurant — street-food stalls, a nightly hippy market, live music, no minimum spend and no booking. Open until midnight. The single best thing within walking distance of your bed.

03
Thursday 3 September · big day out, then FISHER

Formentera by taxi and boat, then Aragma

Taxi door to door at both ends, so no bus timetable governs the day. Ses Illetes genuinely looks like the photographs, and the evening lands back in San Antonio for the best dinner in town.

See this day on the map ↗
  • 08:30

    Taxi from the rank on your own street

    There is an official rank at Carrer des Caló 47 — 92 metres from the hotel door. Taking one off a rank starts the meter at €4.00 instead of €5.45, so walking up beats phoning. About 19 km and 28 minutes to the port.

  • 09:10

    Estació Marítima Eivissa–Formentera

    Avinguda de Santa Eulària. Do not just say “al puerto” — the port is long and there are two maritime stations. The exact words are in the note below.

  • 10:00

    Fast ferry to La Savina

    Trasmapi, Aquabus and Formentera Lines all sail from here, between them every 20–30 minutes. About 30 minutes on a fast boat — check you are not booked on a slow one, which takes up to an hour. ID required to board.

  • 10:30

    L3 to Ses Illetes

    The yellow “Bus Turístic” from the port. Ten minutes, €10 return. The first one of the day is 10:30, so an earlier ferry just means waiting at the port.

  • 11:00

    Ses Illetes

    The headline. Walk the sandbar out towards Espalmador. Arriving by bus or on foot, the natural-park access fee does not apply to you — it is charged per vehicle.

  • 14:00

    Lunch on the sand

    Kiosko El Pirata is the honest one — a hippy beach bar from the early 80s, casual food, famous cocktails. See the traps below before booking Juan y Andrea.

  • 17:45

    Leave the beach early tonight

    The 18:45 is the last L3, but tonight take the 17:45 — it puts you on an earlier ferry, and a boat that lands before 21:00 saves you the night taxi rate on the way home.

  • 19:30

    Taxi back from the port rank

    The rank is 210 m from the terminal door. Land before 21:00 and you are on the day rate: roughly €30–38 for the car rather than €36–46.

  • 20:45

    Aragma

    Modern Greek and Mediterranean mezze, currently rated #1 of 228 restaurants in San Antonio. C/ Madrid 4, Edificio El Faro. Dips €11.50–12.50, the mixed Sabores de Aragma platter €29.95, hot mezze from €10. The kitchen shuts at 23:00, so book the table rather than drifting in.

  • 23:00

    ★ Ítaca · Soul House

    Thirteen minutes on the flat along the front. Free entry, every Thursday 23:00–05:00 all season. Beachfront terrace plus an indoor room on the bay road — and it is on the right side of town for getting home.

The meter is €4.00 to start plus €1.21/km by day, €1.47/km at night. Night rate runs 21:00–07:00 — and, worth knowing for the rest of the week, all day Sunday and from 15:00 on Saturday. Booking by phone or app adds €1.45, which is why the rank beats the app. Luggage is free. The return leg also carries a €2.00 port supplement. Reckon on €65–85 for the two of you across the whole day. An 8% island-wide rise got initial approval in May 2026 and could not be confirmed as in force — if the meter reads a little above these numbers, that is why.
Tell the driver the right terminal, in these words

There are two maritime stations and the wrong one is a 2.5 km, 35-minute walk along a working port dyke. Say: “A la Estación Marítima de Formentera, avenida de Santa Eulària cien.” Not “al puerto”.

The one to avoid is Estació Marítima Eivissa-Botafoc — the big-ferry and cruise terminal, and where a map search is most likely to send a driver by default.

Getting a taxi, and one myth worth killing

The rank two doors down is plan A, and it costs €1.45 less than booking. If it is empty: Radio Taxi Ibiza, +34 971 33 33 33 — phone or WhatsApp, which sidesteps the language barrier entirely; you share your location and get the nearest car. TaxiClick is the only app claiming genuine timed bookings, though its reliability in peak season is questioned.

Uber does not dispatch licensed taxis in Ibiza. It runs as a separate, uncapped service with thin supply and surge pricing. It is not a fallback for the evening — do not plan around it.

Two things worth knowing before you book anything

Juan y Andrea on Ses Illetes is rated around 3.0, with prepaid minimum spends of €120–250 per person depending where they seat you, and €14 water. If you go, book the “random placement” slot — same food, same sand, no minimum. Es Molí de Sal has the same problem.

The L3 timetable is disputed by its own operator. The 2026 poster shows seven round trips starting 10:30; busformentera.com still says every 30 minutes from 09:45. Work to the poster, and check the board at La Savina when you land. Last bus off the beach is 18:45.

The sunset strip is the expensive way to do this

Café Mambo puts a per-person minimum spend on tables after 18:00 — reported at €150 seafront, €100 terrace and €80 in the cocktail area, dropping to about €80 a head after 22:00. A table for two is therefore €160 minimum before you have decided you like it, and cancelling inside four hours is penalised. Café del Mar works the same way and only reveals the figure at checkout.

Those exact figures are second-hand rather than from Mambo itself, so treat them as indicative — but the structure is confirmed, and Ítaca and Soul City are free. If you do want the strip, Savannah is five minutes from Aragma and genuinely rolls into DJ sets, and Golden Buddha is the calmest and cheapest — though it sits 1.5 km from the taxi rank, so don't make it your last stop.

The bus home stops at 23:30

The last P2 to Cala de Bou leaves San Antonio at 23:30, and the bus station is eleven minutes' walk from Aragma — so an all-bus night means leaving dinner by about 23:15. After that there is no bus at all, and the last 3.5 km is a taxi.

Which is fine, and it is the plan: the rank at Passeig de ses Fonts is 3 minutes from Soul City and 6 from Ítaca. Ignore any guide mentioning line U1 — it does not run between 1 May and 30 September. Taxis are scarce at chucking-out time, so join the rank rather than hoping on the street.

Not tonight

Eden has Markus Schulz open-to-close — trance, all night, €40. Es Paradís is running Bubbles, its foam party, €25 with doors at midnight: fun, but you will be soaked, so dress for that rather than for dinner. Neither is worth leaving a free dancefloor for.

04
Friday 4 September · the anchor

Pool day, Calvin Harris, then CamelPhat at Hï

Keep the day flat and shallow — you are inside Ushuaïa from five in the afternoon with no re-entry, and then across the road until six in the morning. Eat before you go in, and know which Hï ticket you are holding, because it decides how much of an evening you get.

See this day on the map ↗
  • 11:00

    Pool, and nothing else

    Hotel pool, or Platja des Xinxó four minutes from the door. Today is about arriving at 17:00 with legs.

  • 14:30

    Light lunch at home

    Do not fill up — the plan is a proper meal on Playa d'en Bossa once you are over there.

  • 15:45

    P2 or a short taxi to the bus station

    Pre-drinks at the hotel or on the Cala de Bou front first: anything you drink here costs a quarter of what it does inside.

  • 16:00

    D3 Discobus → Playa d'en Bossa

    The D3 starts at 16:00, which is exactly what a 17:00 door needs. About 47 minutes, €4 by card.

  • 16:50

    Eat before you go in

    You will be inside for six hours with no re-entry and Ushuaïa's own prices. El Cicheto on Carrer de Porreres is three minutes away, 4.8 from 677 reviews, €20–30 — worth booking on a Calvin Harris Friday. Casa del Mar Bonafé is the beachfront version, but it shuts at 19:30.

  • 17:00

    ★ Calvin Harris · Ushuaïa

    Open-air, with MK, Illyus & Barrientos and Tyson O'Brien warming up. Runs 17:00 to 23:00 — bring sunglasses, it starts in daylight. 18+, original physical ID.

  • 23:05

    Zanzibar, 110 metres away

    Out of Ushuaïa's gate and it is one minute up the road — open until 04:30, €9–21 a head, 4.6 from three and a half thousand reviews. Hot food, a seat, and a drink that is not club-priced. Hï's door is two minutes further on.

  • 00:30

    ★ CamelPhat · Hï Ibiza

    Summer Of Love, with Âme b2b Trikk and Meera in the Theatre, Ewan McVicar and Young Marco in the Club Room. Closes 06:00. No cloakroom at all — one small bag between you.

  • 06:00

    D3 home

    Departures from Playa d'en Bossa run through the night to 06:47. Then a €8 taxi for the last 3.5 km — the P2 stopped hours ago.

You probably have longer than you think — but check the ticket

Ushuaïa ends at 23:00 and Hï's doors open at 23:30, which looks like a thirty-minute scramble. It is not, provided your Hï ticket is the “Entry before 1am” tier. Hï prices in four bands — before midnight €35, before 1am €40–45, standard €50–55, VIP €150 — so a before-1am ticket buys you a genuine ninety minutes between the two venues.

Look at which tier you actually bought. On a before-midnight ticket you have half an hour and should walk straight across. On before-1am you can sit down, eat properly and stroll in at half past midnight.

No re-entry, and no cloakroom

Once you are through Ushuaïa's gate at 17:00 you cannot pop out and come back — that is why the meal goes before rather than during. And Hï has no cloakroom whatsoever: take one small bag between you and nothing you would mind carrying until six in the morning.

Do not rely on the blogs that call Steak 'n Shake “open late” — on a Friday it shuts at midnight. It covers the gap and nothing after it.

Saturday 5 September · the big one

Cala Bassa, sunset at Es Virot, then Bresh

The week handed you a gift. Bresh is the Argentinian party that fuses pop, reggaeton and electronic, and 5 September is the season closing. This is your night.

See this day on the map ↗
  • 11:00

    Sleep in, then the P5

    Slow morning. The P5 leaves San Antonio bus station for Cala Bassa and takes about 34 minutes.

  • 12:30

    Cala Bassa

    Wide, pine-backed and genuinely beautiful — busier and more commercial than the coves up north, but the water is superb. Cala Bassa Beach Club is upscale lounge rather than a party.

  • 18:15

    Leave. Properly, on time.

    This is the one time today that matters — see the note below. Or take the little boat back to San Antonio port instead, which is more fun and lands you in town.

  • 20:00

    Es Virot, for sunset

    Seafront on the Cala de Bou front, walkable from the hotel. Rice dishes are the reason to go. Sunset is 20:20.

  • 23:00

    To San Antonio, then the D1

    The D1 Discobus leaves San Antonio every 30 minutes from 23:30, and Amnesia is 13 minutes up the road at Sant Rafel. Buy your return as you board.

  • 23:45

    ★ Bresh closing party · Amnesia Tickets ✓

    Pop, reggaeton and electronic across the Main Room and the Terraza — the Terraza has a glass roof that floods with light at sunrise, and it is worth being in there at 6am to see it. Amnesia has lockers, unlike Hï, which matters at 6am. Nothing left to book — this one is done.

Runs every day, 1 June–30 September, both directions. This is the single best piece of infrastructure on the island for you — it solves the 4am taxi shortage completely, and almost no visitors use it properly.
Ask the price of the fish. Out loud. Before it's cooked.

Fish sold by the kilo is the most reliable way to get stung in Ibiza — one documented single dish came to €354. Es Virot, Can Pujol and El Gallo Viejo are all excellent and all serve whole fresh fish, so this applies to every one of them. The rice dishes sidestep it entirely.

Bresh or elrow?

Bresh at Amnesia, €50 — a confirmed season closing, your exact music, and the crowd knows every word. It sold out 17 consecutive editions last year, so book it rather than wait; the special guest is deliberately unannounced and may never be revealed.

elrow that night is at UNVRS, not Ushuaïa, and it's €85 with Patrick Topping — a spectacle, but tech-house, which isn't your brief. If you want elrow specifically, the better one is Wednesday at Ushuaïa with Armand Van Helden, outdoors, and it's elrow's last outdoor date of 2026.

Sunday 6 September

Cala Comte, the sunset, and an early night

Recovery day. You finished at 6am yesterday, so this one costs almost nothing and asks almost nothing: no bus before three o'clock, the sunbeds halve their price at 15:30, and the best sunset on the island is free. Home by ten, something cheap near the hotel, bed.

See this day on the map ↗
  • 11:00

    Slowest morning of the trip

    Breakfast, the beach four minutes from the hotel door, a swim, a book. No bus, no plan.

  • 15:00

    P4 to Cala Comte

    Thirty-five minutes from San Antonio bus station. It calls at Cala de Bou on the way out, so you can join it on the bay road rather than going into town first.

  • 15:40

    Arrive, and grab €5 sunbeds

    Sunbeds on Sant Josep beaches are capped at €10 and drop to €5 after 15:30. The day crowd is thinning, the light is getting good, and the water here is the best on the island. Walk the cliff steps west to Cala Escondida for the quiet cove.

  • 19:41

    Golden hour

    The horizon is broken up by islets, so you get the full show rather than a flat line.

  • 20:17

    ★ Sunset over the islands

    This is the one. Down at 20:17, and it keeps going until dusk ends around 20:43. A cold bottle from the Spar on the rocks beats a €40 beach-club table and the view is identical.

  • 21:45

    The last P4

    Do not miss it. Cala de Bou 21:57, San Antonio bus station 22:20. A taxi from out here is about €16 and they don't hang around.

  • 22:00

    Something cheap near the hotel, then bed

    Off the bus at Cala de Bou and eat within a few minutes' walk. Nothing that needs booking, nothing that needs a taxi, nothing that keeps you up.

07
Monday 7 September

Saladeta by boat, then the old town

The clearest water you will see, reached the good way — a forty-minute coastal sail instead of a bus. Then across the island to the only genuinely ancient thing on it.

See this day on the map ↗
  • 10:30

    Boat from Passeig de ses Fonts

    Sea Experience Ibiza run it from the San Antonio waterfront to Cala Salada — about forty minutes up the coast, €9 return, and the run itself is the point.

  • 11:10

    Land at Cala Salada, walk to Saladeta

    Salada has sunbeds, a restaurant and toilets. Then take the rocky path round the back of the cove, ten to fifteen minutes, to Saladeta — the clearest water on the island and absolutely no facilities.

  • 16:45

    Back on the P7, not the boat

    See the note below — the bus runs every 15 minutes until 20:50 and the boat does not. Fifteen minutes into San Antonio, then the bay road home to change.

  • 18:45

    T1 to Ibiza Town

    Every 15 minutes, 45 minutes. Stay on to the Port d'Eivissa stop — a much shorter walk up to the walls than from the bus station.

  • 19:40

    Up through the Portal de ses Taules

    The main gate — a steep stone drawbridge into the walled city. Wear proper shoes: narrow, steep, cobbled, and it is the single most common complaint people have.

  • 19:55

    A drink on the steps at S'Escalinata

    Cushions scattered straight onto the stone steps on the ramp up. The most charming half hour in Ibiza Town, and it is on the way to your table.

  • 20:17

    Sunset from Baluard de Sant Bernat

    The sunset-facing bastion, over Figueretes, Playa d'en Bossa and the salt flats. Free, spectacular, two minutes from dinner.

  • 20:30

    Dinner in Plaça de Vila

    El Olivo Mio — boho, organic, live music, on the prettiest square inside the walls. Your booking is currently for Sunday and needs moving — see below.

  • 22:30

    Carrer de la Verge

    The old town's bar street, just below the walls — about twenty tiny bars, many carved into the rock, and the dancing happens in the lane itself. No ticket, no door charge.

Your El Olivo Mio booking is on the wrong night now

It is held for 20:30 on Sunday 6 September, and the old town has moved to Monday. Ring Plaça de Vila and move it to Monday 7th. Do it early — it is the most photographed square in Dalt Vila and priced accordingly, so tables go.

Saladeta has nothing. Bring everything.

No bar, no toilets, no shade, no lifeguard. Bring water, food and something to sit on, and wear trainers or grippy sandals — the path round is rocky and flip-flops will not do it. Do the walk in daylight, not on the way back at dusk.

Two traps in the old town on a Monday

Teatro Pereyra is in every older guide as the free live-music bar. It is not that any more — it has been rebuilt as a ticketed dinner show. Malanga Café is exactly your music but opens Friday and Saturday only. Don't walk to either.

What is open: Bar 1805 every day 20:00–04:00, food until 02:30. Paradise Lost is the best bet for actually dancing without a ticket, and Plaça del Parc is the locals' square for a quieter one — note Madagascar there is cash only.

If you would rather stay on your own side tonight

Es Paradís is doing "You Know The Vibes" — €25, and it is the only night there this week with real names attached: DJ Policy with Bubba, Danny Beck, Grant Collins and MC Broown. Hip-hop, bashment, afrobeats and reggaeton under the glass pyramid, running until 07:00, and you walk home. The D1 gets you back from Ibiza Town in time if you want both.

The last-mile problem — budget for this

The hotel is in Cala de Bou at the far western end of the bay, about 3.5 km from the bus station: a 45-minute walk or roughly €8 in a taxi. This matters most after midnight, because the Discobus drops you at San Antonio bus station and the P2 stops at 23:30. So every club night ends with a short €8–10 taxi for the final 3 km. Factor roughly €40 across the week.

The upside: demand at the San Antonio rank at 5am is far lower than at the club doors, so it's a quick one to get. Save these now: island-wide WhatsApp +34 971 33 33 33 (share your location) · San Antonio rank +34 971 34 37 64 · Radio Taxi Ibiza +34 971 39 84 83.

02 / AFTER DARK

The four
big ones

Spaced so you never do two 6am finishes back to back — except Friday into Saturday, which is the whole point of the trip.

⚠ The single most important thing on this page

Ushuaïa is an open-air venue with an early licence. Calvin Harris finishes at 23:00. That is an evening show, not a night out — you will be standing on Playa d'en Bossa at 11pm with the night not yet started. Have the "after" decided before you leave the hotel.

The easy answer: Hï Ibiza is directly across the road. Two-minute walk, doors 23:30, €40. No taxi, no queue, no decision at 11pm. If you'd rather have reggaeton, Puro Reggaeton at Es Paradís runs every Friday 23:30–06:00 (€40) and it's back in San Antonio, five minutes from home.

    04 / LOGISTICS

    Getting
    around

    No car, and you won't need one. But Ibiza rebuilt its entire bus network four months ago, so every guide you've read online is wrong.

    ⚠ Read this before you Google anything

    On 1 April 2026 Ibiza scrapped its whole bus system. ALSA took over, and every old line number — L3, L8, L9, L5, L6 — was deleted and replaced with letters: T trunk, P beach, D disco, N night, AERO airport, U/A local. Roughly 100 new buses and about 50% more service.

    Every blog, forum post and travel guide still lists the old numbers. So does the Vibra Riviera's own website. Ignore all of it. The routes below are from the official 2026 ALSA timetables, all valid 1 May–30 Sept, which covers your whole trip. Re-check at alsaibiza.es/en/schedules the week before.

    Always tap a contactless card. Card and QR fares are around 30% cheaper than cash on every line, and the multi-day tourist passes people talk about don't exist — the travel cards are residents-only.

    ⚠ The last-mile problem — budget for this

    The hotel is in Cala de Bou, at the far western end of the bay — not in San Antonio town. It's about 3.5 km from the bus station: a 45-minute walk, or roughly €8 in a taxi. Several booking sites claim "10 minutes to the centre". They're measuring straight across the water.

    This matters most at 5am. The Discobus drops you at San Antonio bus station, not the Bay, and the P2 local bus stops running at midnight. So every club night ends with a short €8–10 taxi for the final 3 km. Factor roughly €40 across the week. The upside: demand for taxis at the San Antonio rank at 5am is far lower than at the club doors, so it's a quick one to get.

    Taxis — numbers, fares, and the 4am reality

    Save these now: island-wide WhatsApp +34 971 33 33 33 (share your location) · San Antonio rank +34 971 34 37 64 · Radio Taxi Ibiza +34 971 39 84 83. Uber works in Ibiza but only dispatches licensed local taxis, so it's the same limited pool of cars. TaxiClick is the local app. You generally cannot pre-book for a specific time — it's dispatch-on-demand.

    Official 2026 fares from San Antonio: Ibiza Town €22.15 day / €26.05 night · Airport €28.20 / €33.40 · Amnesia & UNVRS at Sant Rafel roughly €15–25 · Playa d'en Bossa roughly €30–45. Night rate runs 21:00–07:00, plus from 15:00 on Saturdays and all day Sunday. Real-world tends to run above the calculator.

    The honest warning: Ibiza's taxi licences are capped and club chuck-out demand massively exceeds supply. Expect 30–60 minute waits at Amnesia or Playa d'en Bossa between 04:00 and 06:00, apps showing no cars, and unlicensed touts working the queue. The Discobus is the answer — €3–4, every 30 minutes until 06:30, and it's right there. Buy a return when you board so you're not queuing at the machine at 5am.

    The Cala Comte play — the best-value evening of the week

    Sunbed prices on Sant Josep beaches are capped at €10 a day, and drop to €5 after 15:30. So: catch the 15:30 P4, arrive 16:05, pay €5 each for a bed as the day crowd thins, swim in the best water on the island, watch the sun go down at 20:15, and take the 21:45 bus home at 22:20.

    Total cost, sunbed and both bus fares included: roughly €10–12 each. It fits a late wake-up perfectly, and the light is better in the afternoon anyway. Bring a bottle from the Spar and sit on the rocks — that's the good version. You do not need to book a restaurant to enjoy this sunset.
